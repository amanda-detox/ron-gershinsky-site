Relaxify

Relaxing games: seven quiet little games.
No scores, no timers, nothing to lose.

  • Quiet Night

    Breathe with a glowing circle, or tap to release bubbles and fireflies.

  • Still Pond

    Touch the water for ripples and soft chimes while koi drift by.

  • Glow Garden

    Plant glowing flowers in a dusk meadow and draw your own constellations.

  • Watercolor

    Let soft colors bloom and blend across the paper with your finger.

  • Firefly Jar

    Touch the screen and the fireflies in the jar glow and slowly drift to you.

  • Water Lanterns

    Release glowing paper lanterns that drift slowly across a night lake.

  • Rain on the Window

    Wipe the fogged glass and listen to the rain. Set the rain, lightning and wind yourself.

Sound is off until you tap the speaker.
Take your time.
